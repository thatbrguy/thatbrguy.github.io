I am a CS PhD student at Cornell University, advised by Noah Snavely.

I am interested in creating algorithms that can model the structure, appearance, and dynamics of the world around us from casually captured images and videos. To this end, I typically work at the intersection of learning-based 3D representations, computer vision, and graphics.

Previously, I graduated from the MS in Robotics program at CMU, where I was advised by Shubham Tulsiani. I have also previously interned at Google DeepMind and Intel.

I am always excited to chat about research, especially topics related to 3D computer vision and generative models. Please feel free to reach out!
